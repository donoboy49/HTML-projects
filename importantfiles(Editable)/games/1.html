DELTARUNE Fight Simulator

A free, fan-made battle simulator for DELTARUNE and UNDERTALE fights. It runs in your browser: no download, no account, no ads.

What it is

Pick a fight and play it. The battles are rebuilt one line at a time from the original games' battle code, so the attacks, the ACTs, the spells and the text work the way you remember. You can practise a single attack, replay a whole fight, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.

It is a fan project, made and maintained by one person. It is in beta, so expect a few bugs.

What is in it

  • Fights by chapter. More than 300 encounters: bosses and regular enemies from every DELTARUNE chapter, plus UNDERTALE fights. Every boss has its own page: see all the boss fights.
  • Boss rush. Every boss of a chapter, back to back. There is also a gauntlet and a daily challenge.
  • Autoplay. A bot that dodges for you, to watch a pattern or learn a hard attack.
  • Replays, rewind and practice tools. Rewind a mistake, slow the game down, or drill one attack.
  • Bonus games. Dark World Survivors (a survival mode), Spell Card Rush (a bullet-hell boss rush), blackjack tables, MANIA (a rhythm game), Tetris (dodge falling blocks inside the battle box) and DARKDLE (a daily DELTARUNE guessing game).
  • Works on phones. Touch controls, a gamepad mode, and keyboard controls on a computer.

How to play

  1. Open deltarunesim.com and press Z or Enter (or tap) on the title screen.
  2. Choose a fight from the list. The tabs sort them by chapter; / searches.
  3. In battle, Z confirms, X goes back, and the arrow keys move your SOUL. Dodge the attacks and win by fighting or by sparing.
  4. Press Tab for the toolbox (practice, rewind, autoplay) and ? for every key.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser, with no download, no account and no ads. Any money in it is play money only.

Is this the official DELTARUNE game?

No. It is a fan-made project and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Toby Fox. DELTARUNE and UNDERTALE are (c) Toby Fox. Buy the real games.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It has touch controls. Turn the phone sideways for the best view. On iPhone, Add to Home Screen gives you a full-screen app.

Can I play one specific boss fight online?

Yes. Every boss has its own page on the fights page, and its PLAY THIS FIGHT button opens the simulator with that fight picked. You can also search the fight list with the / key.

Does the site record my fights?

Only anonymously, with no name, account or cookie, and nothing you type. When a fight ends, the simulator sends its result, the hits taken and how long it lasted; the totals are public on the fight stats page. It also sends bug reports that only the developer sees: errors in the game's code, fights that freeze, frame rate with a rough device type (browser and version, phone or computer), files that would not load, and where fights are won, lost or left. To stop sending all of it, turn off Share anonymous stats and bug reports in CONFIG, SYSTEM. Browsers set to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control send nothing.
